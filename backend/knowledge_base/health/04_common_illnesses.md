# Cututtuka Gama-gari a Niger

## Gudawa da Zawo (Diarrhée)

Gudawa shine daya daga cikin manyan musababin mutuwa a tsakanin yara a Niger. Ana iya magance gudawa mai sauki da ORS (Oral Rehydration Solution — ruwan taimako). Yadda ake shirya ORS: sanya gishiri ɗaya (santimita ɗaya) da sukari takwas (santimita takwas) a lita ɗaya na ruwan tafashe wanda ya huce. Sha wannan ruwa da yawa, ɗan kaɗan a kowane 'yan mintuna. Ana ba da ORS kyauta a CSCOM. Idan yaro yana da gudawa tare da amai, ciwon ciki mai tsanani, jini a cikin najasa, ko yana da kwankwasawa — dole a tafi asibiti nan da nan.

## Zazzaɓin Zuciya (Méningite)

Meningitis tana yaɗuwa a yankin Sahel a cikin "Meningitis Belt" wanda Niger yake ciki, musamman a lokacin rani (Fabrairu zuwa Mayu). Alamomi: ciwon kai mai tsanani sosai, zazzaɓi mai ƙarfi, wuyan hannu ba ya motsi da sauƙi (raideur de nuque), tsuruwa a cikin haske (photophobie), amai, da wani lokaci fitar jini ƙarƙashin fata (taches violacées). Meningitis cuta ce mai haɗari da ke kashe mutane cikin sa'o'i kalla 24. Idan kuna ganin waɗannan alamomi, ku tafi asibiti nan take — koda dare.

## Kyanda (Rougeole — Measles)

Kyanda cuta ce ta ƙurjin da ke yaɗuwa cikin sauƙi ta hanyar numfashi. Ita ce daya daga cikin cututtuka mafi kashe yara a Niger. Alamomi: zazzaɓi, hanci mai gudana, idanu masu ja da hawaye, sannan bayan kwana 3 zuwa 4 ƙurjin ya fara bayyana a fuska ya shimfiɗa duk jikin. Rigakafin kyanda (vaccin anti-rougeoleux) ana ba da shi kyauta a CSCOM a cikin taron allurar rigakafin yara. Duk yaro ya kamata ya sami allurar kyanda a watanni 9 da kuma a shekara ɗaya. Idan yaro ya kamu da kyanda kuma yana da wahalar numfashi ko baya iya cin abinci, ku tafi asibiti.

## Tari da Matsalar Numfashi (IRA — Infections Respiratoires Aiguës)

Tarin tare da wahalar numfashi yana daya daga cikin manyan cututtukan yara a ƙarƙashin shekara 5. Alamomi masu haɗari: numfashi mai sauri sosai (fiye da 50 a minti ga yara 'yan wata 2 zuwa shekara 1), ko numfashi mai sauri fiye da 40 a minti ga yara shekara 1 zuwa 5, ko ƙirjin yana ja ya ja a lokacin numfashi (tirage sous-costal). Waɗannan alamomin na nuna cuta mai tsanani (pneumonie) wacce ke buƙatar maganin antibiotic a CSCOM ko asibiti nan da nan.

## Cutar Zazzaɓi mai Zubar Jini (Méningococcémie / Fièvre hémorragique)

Idan mutum yana da zazzaɓi tare da tabo na jini a ƙarƙashin fata (taches rouges ko violacées masu kama da rauni), ya zama alamar hatsari mafi tsanani. Wannan na iya zama cutar meningococcémie ko ta irin ta, kuma tana buƙatar magani a asibiti nan take. Kada ku jira ku ga injirar ko ma'aikacin lafiya.
