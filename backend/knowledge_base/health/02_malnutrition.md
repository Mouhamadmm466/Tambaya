# Rashin Abinci Mai Gina Jiki (Malnutrition)

Niger na daya daga cikin ƙasashen da ke fama da rashin abinci mai gina jiki sosai a duniya. Yara 'yan ƙasa da shekara 5 suna cikin haɗarin mafi girma, musamman a lokacin ƙarancin abinci (Maris zuwa Yuni) kafin girbin sabon amfanin gona.

## Alamomin Rashin Abinci a Yara

Alamomi da ke nuna yaro na fama da rashin abinci mai gina jiki: hannaye da ƙafafu masu ƙyaura da rauni, ciki mai kumburi (ƙwallon ruwa a ciki), gashin kai ya zama na launin ja ko rawaya, fata ta yi bushe da kwarkwata, yaro baya son ci abinci ko ruwansa ya kau, kuma yaro ba ya girma daidai da shekararsa.

## Gwajin Muac — Yadda Ake Auna Lafiyar Yaro

MUAC shine auna girth na hannun hagu a tsakiya tsakanin kafaɗa da gwiwa. A cibiyoyin lafiya ana amfani da rigar roba ta launi (MUAC tape). Idan launi ya zama ja, yaron yana cikin haɗarin mutuwa (kwayar rashin abinci mai tsanani — MAM ko SAM), dole a kai shi CRENAS ko CRENI nan take. Idan launi ya zama rawaya, yaron yana buƙatar kulawa da magani. Idan launi ya zama kore, yaron yana da lafiya.

## Cibiyoyin Maganin Rashin Abinci a Niger

CRENAS (Centres de Réhabilitation Nutritionnelle Ambulatoire pour Sévère) suna wanzu a cikin yawancin CSCOM a faɗin Niger. Ana ba da Plumpy'Nut (abinci na musamman mai dauke da mai da sunadarai) ga yara masu fama da rashin abinci mai tsanani kyauta. CRENI (Centres de Réhabilitation Nutritionnelle Intensive) yana cikin asibitoci na gundumar (hôpitaux de district) don yara masu haɗari sosai. Ku tafi CSCOM ko asibiti kusa da ku ku nemi gwajin MUAC ga yaro.

## Rigakafin Rashin Abinci

Shayar da nono kaɗai (allaitement exclusif) har yaro ya cika watanni 6 yana kare shi daga rashin abinci da cututtuka da yawa. Bayan watanni 6, fara saka abinci mai gina jiki tare da nono: gweba (légumes), wake, hatsi, mai. Noman lambu a kusa da gida yana taimakawa wajen samun abinci mai gina jiki gajere a duk shekara. Ɗaukar nauyin yaro a CSCOM kowane wata yana taimakawa gano matsalar da wuri.

## Cutar Kwashiorkor da Marasmus

Kwashiorkor: yaro yana da kumburi a ƙafafu da wajensa, fata mai rauni da kwarkwata, nishi. Wannan matsala ce mai tsanani wacce ke buƙatar maganin asibiti nan da nan. Marasmus: yaro ya yi ƙyaura sosai, ƙasusuwa suna bayyana, babu kitse a jikin yaro. Dukansu suna buƙatar maganin CRENAS ko CRENI.
