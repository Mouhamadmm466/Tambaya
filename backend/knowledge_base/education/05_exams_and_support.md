# Jarrabawar Ƙasa da Tallafi ga Ɗalibai a Niger

## CFEPD — Jarrabawar Ƙarshen Firamare

CFEPD (Certificat de Fin d'Études du Premier Degré) shine jarrabawar da ake yi a ƙarshen makaranta ta firamare (bayan CM2). Duk ɗalibi da ya cika shekara 6 na firamare na iya yi wa wannan jarrabawa. Jarrabawar CFEPD tana ƙunshe da: Faransanci (karatu, rubutu, nahawu), Lissafi (calcul), da sauran darussa. Ana yin CFEPD a watan Yuni kowace shekara. Ɗalibai da suka ci CFEPD za su iya shiga kolej (CES ko collège). Waɗanda suka faɗi suna iya sake yin jarrabawar shekara mai zuwa.

## BEPC — Jarrabawar Ƙarshen Kolej

BEPC (Brevet d'Études du Premier Cycle) ana yin sa a ƙarshen Troisième (shekara ta 4 ta kolej). Darussa na BEPC sun haɗa da: Faransanci, Lissafi, Kimiyya (SVT da physique), Tarihi da Geography, da wasu lokuta Larabci ko Ingilishi. Ɗalibai da suka ci BEPC za su iya shiga lise. BEPC takardar shaidar ilimi ce ta farko wacce gwamnati ta amince da ita kuma ana buƙatarsa don neman wasu ayyukan gwamnati.

## BAC — Baccalauréat

BAC (Baccalauréat) shine jarrabawa mafi ɗaukaka a makarantar sakandare a Niger. Ana yin BAC a ƙarshen Terminale (shekara ta ƙarshe ta lise). Nau'in BAC a Niger sun haɗa da: BAC A (Lettres et Sciences humaines), BAC C (Mathématiques et Sciences physiques), BAC D (Biologie), da BAC G (Gestion da kasuwanci). Ɗalibai da suka ci BAC za su iya shiga jami'a a Niger ko kasashen waje.

## Tallafi da Ɗalibai Suka Cancanta (Bourses)

Gwamnatin Niger tana ba da tallafi (bourse scolaire) ga ɗalibai masu nasara amma marasa kuɗi don ci gaba da karatu a kolej, lise, ko jami'a. Don neman tallafi, je ofishin ilimi na gundumar da takardun: CFEPD ko BEPC ko BAC, takardar haihuwa, da takarda ta tabbatar da yanayin tattalin arzikin iyali. Ƙungiyoyi kamar UNICEF, Aga Khan, da wasu ofisoshin addini suna kuma ba da tallafi.

## Shirye-shiryen Karatu na Musamman (Soutien Scolaire)

Wasu ƙungiyoyi masu zaman kansu da ofisoshin addini suna ba da darussan ƙarin karatu (cours de soutien) kyauta ko da kuɗi kaɗan ga ɗalibai a gaban jarrabawa. Iyaye su nemi irin waɗannan shirye-shirye a unguwarsu. Malaman makaranta suna kuma iya ba da ƙarin lokaci bayan makaranta don taimakon ɗalibai masu wahala.

## Jami'a a Niger

Jami'ar Abdou Moumouni ta Niamey (UAM) ita ce jami'ar gwamnati mafi girma a Niger. Akwai kuma Université Islamique de Say da wasu kolejoyin sana'a da fasaha. Ɗalibai da suka ci BAC za su iya shiga jami'a ta hanyar sanya sunan (inscription) a makarantar da suka zaɓa. Akwai shirye-shiryen tallafi na gwamnati da kuma na ƙasashen waje (France, Morocco, Senegal, Algeria) don ɗalibai masu nasara.
