# Geography da Yankuna na Niger

## Ganaral Bayani kan Niger

Niger ƙasa ce da ke a Yammacin Afirka, ba ta da teku (landlocked). Niger ta yi iyaka da: Algeria da Libya (arewa), Chad (gabas), Nigeria da Benin (kudu), Burkina Faso da Mali (yamma). Niamey ita ce babban birnin ƙasar Niger kuma ita ce mafi girma a cikin biranen Niger. Nijar Riba (Fleuve Niger) yana gudana ta yammacin ƙasar, yana ba da ruwa ga Niamey da yankunan kusa da kasa.

## Yankunan Niger Takwas

Niger tana da yankuna (régions) takwas: Agadez (mafi girma cikin fili — kusan kashi ɗaya bisa uku na ƙasar), Diffa (kudu-maso-gabas, iyaka da Chad da Nigeria), Dosso (kudu-maso-yamma, kusa da Benin da Burkina Faso), Maradi (kudu-tsakiya, mafi yawan yawan jama'a), Tahoua (tsakiya-arewa), Tillabéri (yamma, kusa da Niamey), Zinder (kudu-maso-gabas, birnin tarihi), da Niamey mai musamman (Communauté Urbaine de Niamey).

## Manyan Biranen Niger

Birnin Niamey shine babban birni, ya na a yamma, a bakin Kogin Nijar. Zinder shine birnin tarihi mafi girma na biyu a Niger, ya na kudu-maso-gabas. Maradi shine cibiyar kasuwanci mafi mahimmanci, kusa da iyakar Nigeria. Tahoua shine cibiyar yankin tsakiya mai muhimmanci ga noma da kasuwanci. Agadez shine birni na hamada, mashahuri da shimfidar turawa (tourisme) da hakar ma'adinai (uranium).

## Kogin Nijar (Fleuve Niger)

Kogin Nijar yana gudana kilomita 550 a cikin Niger ta yankin yammacin ƙasar daga Mali zuwa Nigeria. Kogin yana wucewa ta Niamey kuma yana ba da ruwan shayarwa, kiwo, da kamun kifi ga miliyoyin mutane. Yankin da ke kusa da kogin (vallée du Niger) shine mafi arziki a noma a cikin ƙasar.

## Hamada ta Ténéré da Arewacin Niger

Arewacin Niger galibi hamada ce ta Sahara. Yankin Ténéré a cikin yankin Agadez shine ɗaya daga cikin hamadodi mafi girma a Afirka. Ana hakar uranium a Arlit da Imouraren a yankin Agadez, kuma wannan ma'adini yana da muhimmanci sosai ga tattalin arzikin Niger.

## Nisan Tsakanin Biranen

Daga Niamey zuwa Maradi kusan kilomita 650. Daga Niamey zuwa Zinder kusan kilomita 900. Daga Niamey zuwa Agadez kusan kilomita 900. Daga Niamey zuwa Dosso kusan kilomita 135. Manyan tituna (routes nationales) su ne hanyar zirga-zirga ta ƙasa.
